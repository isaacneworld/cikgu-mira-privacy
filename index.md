Game progress, stars, language choices, and settings are stored locally on the device. Local data may be removed when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

This version contains no ads, analytics, in-app purchases, or chat features. The app does not request location, camera, or microphone access. If features such as purchases or ads are added later, this policy and Google Play declarations will be updated before those features are released.

For questions or requests about children's privacy, contact **nazrulbluep@gmail.com**. Parents can delete locally stored data through device settings or by uninstalling the app.
